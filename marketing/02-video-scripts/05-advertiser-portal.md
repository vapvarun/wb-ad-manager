**[NARRATOR]**

## How the Portal Works
**[TIME: 0:50-1:30]**

**[NARRATOR]**
"Here's the flow from the advertiser's perspective."

**Step 1: Registration**
"They register for an advertiser account - either automatically when they sign up for your site, or by request."

**[SHOW]**
Registration form

**Step 2: Add Funds**
"They add funds to their wallet through your WooCommerce shop or membership plugin checkout — using whatever payment gateway you already have configured."

**[SHOW]**
Add funds interface

**Step 3: Create Ads**
"They create their own ads - upload images, write copy, choose placements."

**[SHOW]**
Ad creation form

**Step 4: Submit**
"They submit for your approval. You get a notification, review it, and approve or reject."

**[SHOW]**
Approval notification

**Step 5: Live**
"Once approved, the ad goes live and credits are deducted from their wallet automatically."

Profile form
