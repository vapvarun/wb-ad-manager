# Use Case: Classifieds Marketplace Mode

**For:** Site owners who want to build a niche buy/sell marketplace, job board, local directory, or any "users post listings" platform.

**One-sentence promise:** Users post their listings, pay for featured placements from their wallet, and you moderate and collect — all built into WordPress with no custom development.

---

![Classifieds browse page — public listings with filters](../../docs/website/images/pro/classifieds-browse.png)

---

## What you get

- **Frontend submission forms** — no admin access needed. Users fill in title, description, price, images, and contact details on a public page. You stay out of their workflow until moderation.
- **Paid upgrades that sell themselves** — Featured ($29.99), Highlighted, Urgent/Bump — each charges credits from the seller's wallet at the moment they choose the upgrade. You configure the prices; the system handles the rest.
- **Membership plans for power sellers** — create Starter, Pro, and Agency tiers that determine how many listings a seller can publish and how many featured upgrades they get per month. Membership plans live at `Classifieds → Membership Plans` in your admin.

---

## This is for you if…

You want to launch a local jobs board, a real estate listings site, a community buy/sell group, or any niche marketplace where user-generated listings are the product. The model works with any category: pets, tools, gigs, cars, events.

Sellers pay from a credit wallet. Credits reach the wallet through your WooCommerce shop or membership plugin (Paid Memberships Pro or MemberPress) — the bundled Wbcom Credits SDK adapter handles the connection automatically.
