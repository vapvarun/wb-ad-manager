# Use Case: Paid Ad Portal Mode

**For:** Sites that run a full open ad marketplace — multiple advertisers buying campaign budgets and tracking performance in real time.

**One-sentence promise:** Run a professional ad network on your WordPress site — campaign budgets, CPM/CPC pricing, A/B testing, and a full analytics dashboard — without building it from scratch.

---

![Advertiser Portal — Campaigns tab with active CPM and CPC campaigns](../../docs/website/images/pro/portal-campaigns.png)

---

## What you get

- **Full campaign lifecycle** — advertisers activate campaigns with a budget; Pro pre-charges the full amount from their wallet, runs the campaign, and refunds any unspent credits when it completes. No surprise billing, no runaway spend.
- **CPM, CPC, or flat-rate packages** — price what you sell the way your advertisers expect. Mix pricing models across different packages for different placements.
- **Analytics advertisers can trust** — each advertiser sees their own impressions, clicks, and CTR inside the portal. You see everything on the revenue dashboard. No screenshots, no manual reports.

---

## This is for you if…

You run a community, media site, or directory with enough traffic that multiple advertisers want to compete for placements. You want them to run budgeted campaigns, not just static banners, and you want the whole thing to run with minimal admin time on your end.
