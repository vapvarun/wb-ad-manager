# Use Case: Sponsored Content Mode

**For:** Publishers who sell ad space directly to sponsors and want to automate the process.

**One-sentence promise:** Stop chasing sponsors over email — let them create accounts, submit ads, fund their own wallet, and track their own results while you approve in one click.

---

![Advertiser Portal — Ads tab showing live sponsored campaigns](../../docs/website/images/pro/portal-ads.png)

---

## What you get

- **Self-service advertiser portal** — sponsors register, design their ad, pick a package, and submit. You get a notification. You review and click Approve. That's your entire workload per campaign.
- **Wallet-based billing** — credits flow into each sponsor's wallet through your existing WooCommerce shop or membership plugin (via the bundled Wbcom Credits SDK adapters). No invoicing, no chasing payments. When an ad runs, credits deduct automatically.
- **Packages you define** — set flat-rate, CPM, or CPC packages at different price points. Basic sidebar, premium in-content, featured placement — all at prices you choose.

---

## This is for you if…

You have a site with real traffic and local businesses or niche brands asking about sponsorships. Right now you manage it all by hand — email, invoices, manual ad setup. You want to stop trading hours for every sale and instead build a system sponsors can use themselves.
